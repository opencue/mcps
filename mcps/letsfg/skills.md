Use when user asks to "find flights", "search flights", "book a flight", "compare airline prices", "find cheap flights", "fly from X to Y", "find connections", "find layover options", or any flight-related travel query. Do NOT use for hotel-only searches, car rentals, or non-flight travel bookings. |
